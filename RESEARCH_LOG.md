# SENTIENCE Research Log

## 2026-08-30 — Project established

The SENTIENCE repository was created and the project charter was established in `README.md`.

Initial research position: the project will investigate increasingly sophisticated artificial cognitive properties without assuming that any particular behavior constitutes proof of sentience.

## 2026-08-30 — Engineering scaffold established

The first engineering instructions and initial architecture were added. The project is intentionally modular and conservative at this stage. No autonomous agency, continuous operation, self-modification, or unrestricted external access is being implemented yet.

### Initial hypothesis

A persistent modular cognitive architecture combining memory, self-modeling, world modeling, metacognition, motivation, learning, and controlled agency may provide a stronger experimental basis for investigating artificial sentience than a standalone conversational model.

### Important limitation

This is a hypothesis, not an established scientific conclusion. Future experiments must attempt to falsify it.
